About

I am a PhD student in the Department of Political Science at Texas A&M University. My research focuses on authoritarian politics, civil-military relations, and conflict. My dissertation, Authoritarian Leader Survival in Conflict, is supervised by Matthew Fuhrmann (chair) and Hyeran Jo (co-chair).

Before coming to Texas A&M, I received an M.A. in International Relations from Chung-Ang University and a B.A. in Public Administration from Kyonggi University in Seoul.
