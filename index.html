About

Hello and thanks for visiting my website! My name is Hongbi Choi, and I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Political Science at Texas A&M University. My reserach focuses on authoritarian leader survival and political violence. Specifically, I examine how authoritarian leaders' threat perception shapes their responses to domestic unrest and interstate conflict. I also maintain active side projects on civil war and rebel governance.
